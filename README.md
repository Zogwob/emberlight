# Emberlight — Keep the Last Lantern Lit

A 2D arena-survival game built in plain JavaScript (canvas, no engine).
Move with WASD / arrows, aim with the mouse, click or hold Space to shoot
light bolts at the shadows. Survive 10 waves, pick an upgrade card between
waves, and keep the last lantern lit — its light shrinks as your health drops.
Clear wave 10 and dawn breaks; then endless mode begins.

**Play:** https://zogwob.github.io/emberlight/

Made for the make.fun bounty **best-game-01** ("Make the best game") as
@zogwob — entry "Emberlight", submitted 16 Nov 2026. All art and sound are
procedural (code-drawn visuals, WebAudio SFX); no assets copied, no network.
